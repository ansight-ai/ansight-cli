Included dependencies:
- FFmpeg 7.1.1: LGPL 2.1 or later (shared libraries, no GPL options enabled).
- libusb 1.0.29: LGPL 2.1 or later (shared library).
- SDL 2.32.8: zlib license (shared library).
- dav1d 1.5.0: BSD 2-Clause (linked into FFmpeg).
- Android platform-tools 36.0.0: see Android-platform-tools-NOTICE.txt.

Corresponding scrcpy, FFmpeg, libusb, SDL and dav1d source archives are included
in ../sources. The scrcpy source includes the upstream build recipes in
release/build_windows.sh and app/deps, including compiler and configure options.
The binary and source archive SHA-256 pins are maintained in Ansight's
scripts/package/bundle-scrcpy.proj. No modifications were made to those binaries.
You may replace the shared libraries and exercise the modification, debugging,
and reverse-engineering rights granted by their respective licenses. Ansight's
bundle consistency check requires updating bundle.json when replacing files;
an independently installed scrcpy can also be selected explicitly.

Android notice provenance: platform-tools_r36.0.0-win.zip from
https://dl.google.com/android/repository/platform-tools_r36.0.0-win.zip
SHA-256: 12c2841f354e92a0eb2fd7bf6f0f9bf8538abce7bd6b060ac8349d6f6a61107c
The scrcpy v3.3.1 build recipe selects platform-tools 36.0.0. Google has since
re-signed that archive; executable code is unchanged, while PE checksums and
Authenticode signature bytes differ from the copies in the scrcpy release.
